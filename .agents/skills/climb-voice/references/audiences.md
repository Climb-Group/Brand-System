# Audiences

Climb27 marketing is led by audience, not by event: each group gets its own messages, page, emails and tone. Priority order: scaling companies, professional services and companies seeking investment first; then universities and investors; the public sector, incubators and accelerators, advisers and fractional leaders matter most as partners who bring their own networks. People who have been before come first in every campaign: they should be buying now.

**Attendee pages and partner pages are separate.** A /for/ page speaks to people who come with a ticket: what they get, who they meet, which ticket. Selling a stand, a session or a package to the same kind of organisation belongs on a partner page (the partner page can be split by segment). Do not mix the two on one page.

## Ticket types in plain words

- **Start-up ticket:** for businesses at an early stage, including pre-revenue, as long as they are making money, however little, and will benefit from growing the business. Do not write "pre-revenue" on its own.
- **Professional ticket:** for anyone established.
- **Team tickets:** 25% off. Sell the reasons the team should come (each role gets sessions built for their job), then the offer.

## Shared truths every audience can hear

- Two days, every session, the grand opening and the after parties.
- Business that feels like a festival: Find Your People and Main Stage Moments.
- Close and personal: the right people, not the most people.
- A community: people who share what they have learned and send each other work.
- Practical growth you can act on the next morning.
- Three in four people who come leave with follow-ups that matter: a sale, a partnership, an investment or funding. (Never "at least three".)
