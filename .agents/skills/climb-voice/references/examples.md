# Examples

## Lines that work

- Your business. Bigger stage.
- Find your people. Then do business with them.
- Iconic moments. Real advice. No fluff.
- Two days. The right rooms. Conversations that move your business.
- Deal flow you can filter. Founders you choose.
- Business that feels like a festival.

## Paragraphs that work

> Climb27 is two days built around the conversations that move a business forward. Founders, investors and the advisers who back them, in rooms designed for meeting, not milling about.

> Main Stage Moments are short and big: the announcements, the awards, the sets that turn a business event into a night people talk about. Then it is back to the work, with the people you came to meet.

> Raising this year? Spend day one getting your story investor-ready with people who have done it, and day two in the conversations that count.

## Rewrites

| Before | Problem | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Join thousands of founders at the UK's premier startup event! | Size claim, unsupported superlative, exclamation mark | Two days with the founders, investors and advisers who move businesses forward. |
| "The best business event this year so far" - Johnny J Pawlik, Mantra Media Ltd, Climb24 | The edition dates the quote and makes the festival look old | "The best business event this year so far" - Johnny J Pawlik, Mantra Media Ltd |
| Climb is back - and bigger than ever | Bare "Climb", dash, size claim | Climb27 is here. Your business, on a bigger stage. |
| Guaranteed meetings with 50+ investors | Guarantee, number, investor-access promise | Find investors looking for real deal flow. |
| Hear inspiring stories from successful entrepreneurs | Inspiration, not doing | Learn how they grew, and what you can do about it on Monday. |
| Location TBC, coming soon | Hedge words, venue hint | (Leave the venue out until the announcement. Sell the experience.) |
| Exhibit and reach thousands of attendees | Audience size | Put your team in front of the businesses that buy what you do. |
| Our platform, powered by Cvent, makes booking easy | Vendor name | Book your meetings before you arrive. |

## From the sales and partner review (October 2026)

| Before | Problem | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Raising? Meet investors with your eyes open | Reads as a warning | Raising? Find investors looking for real deal flow |
| Investors who came to meet you. | Over-promises, and puts the founder at the centre | Investors here to find their next deal. |
| Investors: cheque books, portfolio experience and a seat on the stage. They attend to meet companies, not to speak. | Jargon, and an offer investors do not want | Investors: capital and portfolio experience. They come for deal flow: companies already trading. |
| Advisers: the hard-won detail, shared in roundtables and workshops. Looking for clients with a live problem and a budget to solve it. | Budget line is not needed; reads like professional services firms | Advisers: the hard-won detail, shared in roundtables, on stages and in workshops. They give the advice; professional services firms implement it. |
| a year of lessons from the same problems you have, learned the expensive way. | Needs the peer framing | Fellow founders one step ahead, with a year of lessons from the same problems, learned the expensive way. |
| Evidence of growth in their patch. | Slang, and sounds parochial | Evidence of growth across their region. |
| Three in four people leave with at least three follow-ups that matter. | Over-precise | Three in four people who come leave with follow-ups that matter. |
| Can I bring the team? | Asks permission instead of selling | Why your team should come (then: team tickets are 25% off). |
| I bought a one-day ticket. Which day do I pick? | Written for someone who already bought | I want a one-day ticket. Which day should I pick? |
| Will you introduce me to people? | "Introduce" promises a personal service | How will I connect with the right people? |
| What Climb27 does for an advisory firm | A heading with no benefit | New clients, from the growing businesses you want to serve |
| New clients, with a return you can show | Return on investment does not belong in the headline | New clients. (The return goes in the body.) |
| Investor Speed Networking: six founders matched to your thesis, two at a time | Times, counts and "matched" | Investor Speed Networking: founders in your sector, brought to you. |
| Councils and combined authorities | Too narrow | The public sector |
| Climb27 is doubling down on referrals. | Referral sounds like a favour | Join the Climb27 Affiliate Programme. |
| Book a call (in "Still deciding?") | Says nothing about why | Interested in partnering? Book a call with the team. |
| When is the programme published? (then a paragraph on why agendas date) | Wrong word, answer buried | When is the agenda published? A few weeks before the event, once every session is confirmed. Here is why we wait. |

## ClimbVoice, corrected

The 2025 ClimbVoice prompt is the root of this skill. Two things changed:

- Its example paragraphs use em dashes, which the brand bans. Use full stops or commas.
- It is written founder-first. Climb27 is audience-led: keep the festival energy for everyone, and use the tone note for the audience you are writing for.
