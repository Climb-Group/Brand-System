## Page 4

<!-- @body.h1 -->
Radio

<!-- @body.h2 -->
Callsigns

<!-- @body.p -->
First names, plus Control and Security. Say who you are calling first, then who you are: "Control, from [your name]". Control answers "Go ahead".

<!-- @body.h2 -->
General phrases

<!-- @body.table widths=5.0,11.6 -->
| Say | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Standby or Wait one | I heard you; give me a moment |
| So far | Have you understood so far? |
| Check | Understood |
| Priority | This needs attention next |
| Urgent | Stop other traffic; this is urgent |

<!-- @body.h2 -->
Emergency phrases

<!-- @body.table widths=5.0,11.6 -->
| Say | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Mr Sands | A possible fire |
| Inspector Gadget | An unattended or suspicious item |
| PANIC | A life-threatening incident |

<!-- @body.callout -->
Never say fire, bomb or suspicious device on the radio. Use the phrase, give the location, and wait for Control.

<!-- @body.h1 -->
Escalation

<!-- @body.numbers -->
1. Who: your team lead, in person.
2. What: say what happened, where and who is involved.
3. Solution: say what you have already done.
4. Urgency: say how fast it needs an answer.
5. No team lead nearby: use the nearest radio and say "Requesting Control".

<!-- @body.h1 -->
Emergency procedures

<!-- @body.bullets -->
- Fire or evacuation: call it on the radio as Mr Sands with the location. When the evacuation announcement plays, guide people to the nearest exit and the assembly point at [location].
- Unwell or injured: stay with the person, call Control for first aid, do not move them.
- Threatening behaviour: do not engage. Call Security on the radio with the location and a description.
- Safeguarding: a missing child or vulnerable adult is called to Control at once with a description and where they were last seen. Nobody leaves through the doors until Control says so.
- Afterwards: record every incident in the log at the back of this pack, the same day.
