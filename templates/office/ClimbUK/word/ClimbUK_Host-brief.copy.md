<!-- @body.h1 -->
Preparing your session

<!-- @body.bullets -->
- Your host sets up a group chat for each session, named "[Edition] - Day, Stage, Time", with the team's event mobile added, and books a video call before the event.
- A session with several guests runs like a TV chat show: a 5 minute host introduction, then the conversation.
- A one-to-one is a fireside chat.
- Please do not script it. Know your three points and talk.

<!-- @body.h2 -->
Promote your session

<!-- @body.p -->
The share kit has images and lines for your social channels. Post once when the agenda goes live and once the week before.

<!-- @body.h1 -->
On the day

<!-- @body.bullets -->
- Registration opens at [time]. Bring your e-ticket or use the ClimbGroup Event App.
- Arrive at the speaker green room at least one hour before your session, and allow 30 minutes before that to collect your pass.
- No pass, no stage access.

<!-- @body.h2 -->
Ending your session

<!-- @body.p -->
60 seconds before the end a tone plays and music fades in. Wrap up in those 60 seconds. The lights dim, you leave the stage and hand back the microphones straight away. A session that overruns has its microphones cut, so the next one starts on time.

<!-- @body.h2 -->
The green room

<!-- @body.p -->
For speakers, hosts and partners only. Capacity is limited for fire safety, so guests wait in the lounge.
