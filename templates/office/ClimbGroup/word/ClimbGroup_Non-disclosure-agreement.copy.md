This is a mutual agreement: each party may share confidential information with the other for the purpose above, and each keeps what it receives confidential.

<!-- @body.clauses start=1 -->
1. Definitions: What counts as confidential information, and what the purpose is.
2. Confidential information: Everything marked confidential, and anything a reasonable person would treat as confidential, in any form.
3. Obligations of the receiving party: Use it only for the purpose, share it only with people who need it and are bound the same way, and keep it secure.
4. Exceptions: Information that is public, already known, independently developed or required by law.
5. Return or destruction: On request or at the end of the term, return or destroy it and confirm in writing.
6. Term: How long the obligations last, including after the agreement ends.
7. Remedies: Each party may seek an injunction as well as damages.
8. General: Whole agreement, variation, assignment, notices, law and jurisdiction.
