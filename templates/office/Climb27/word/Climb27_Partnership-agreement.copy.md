<!-- @body.p -->
This agreement sets out what the Partner books, what ClimbGroup provides and what each side does. Clause headings are fixed; the approved wording for each clause goes under its heading.

<!-- @body.clauses start=1 -->
1. Definitions and interpretation: What each capitalised term means, and how to read the agreement.
2. Fees and payment: The fee in Schedule 1, invoiced on signature and payable within 14 days of the invoice date.
3. Each party's obligations: What each side does, by when, and the information each gives the other.
4. Host obligations: Promote the partner, feature their mark, keep them informed and co-operate. No particular result is assured.
5. Changes: A change is sent on the change request form, reviewed within 48 hours, and none is made within 72 hours of the event. At most 5 changes.
6. Limitation of liability: What each party is and is not liable for, and the cap.
7. Termination: When either party may end the agreement, and the notice needed.
8. Cancellation by the partner: The sliding scale in the table below applies to written notice.
9. Consequences of termination: What is paid, returned and taken down when the agreement ends.
10. Anti-bribery and compliance: Each party complies with the Bribery Act 2010 and keeps its own policies.
11. Intellectual property and use of marks: Each party keeps its own marks and licenses them only for this event.
12. Data protection: How attendee and contact data is handled under UK GDPR, and who is the controller.
13. Force majeure: What happens when something outside either party's control stops the event.
14. General: Whole agreement, variation, waiver, assignment, notices, third parties, law and jurisdiction.
