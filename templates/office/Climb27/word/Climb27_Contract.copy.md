<!-- @body.p -->
This agreement sets out the services, the fees and what each side does. Clause headings are fixed; the approved wording for each clause goes under its heading.

<!-- @body.clauses start=1 -->
1. Definitions and interpretation: What each capitalised term means, and how to read the agreement.
2. Term: When the agreement starts and ends, and how it renews.
3. Services: What is supplied, to what standard, and by when (Schedule 1).
4. Fees and payment: The fees in Schedule 2, invoiced [monthly] and payable within 14 days of the invoice date.
5. Each party's obligations: What each side does and the information each gives the other.
6. Changes: How a change to the services or the fees is requested, reviewed and agreed in writing.
7. Limitation of liability: What each party is and is not liable for, and the cap.
8. Termination: When either party may end the agreement, and the notice needed.
9. Consequences of termination: What is paid, returned and handed over when the agreement ends.
10. Anti-bribery and compliance: Each party complies with the Bribery Act 2010 and keeps its own policies.
11. Intellectual property: Who owns what is created, and the licence each party gets.
12. Confidentiality: What stays confidential, for how long, and the exceptions.
13. Data protection: How personal data is handled under UK GDPR, and who is the controller.
14. Force majeure: What happens when something outside either party's control stops performance.
15. General: Whole agreement, variation, waiver, assignment, notices, third parties, law and jurisdiction.
