Risk assessment

<!-- @body.p -->
Score likelihood 1 to 5 (1 Rare, 2 Unlikely, 3 Possible, 4 Likely, 5 Almost certain) and severity 1 to 5 (1 Minor injury or inconvenience, 2 First aid required, 3 Injury requiring medical treatment, 4 Serious injury, 5 Major injury or fatality).
