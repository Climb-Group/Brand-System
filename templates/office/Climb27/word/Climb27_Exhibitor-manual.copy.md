Health and safety acknowledgement

<!-- @body.p -->
By signing, you confirm that your company and everyone working on your stand will follow the law below, the site rules and the instructions of the event team.

<!-- @body.bullets -->
- Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974
- Management of Health and Safety at Work Regulations 1999
- Workplace (Health, Safety and Welfare) Regulations 1992
- Provision and Use of Work Equipment Regulations (PUWER) 1998
- Work at Height Regulations 2005
- Reporting of Injuries, Diseases and Dangerous Occurrences Regulations (RIDDOR) 2013
- Control of Substances Hazardous to Health (COSHH) Regulations 2002
- Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Regulations
- Manual Handling Operations Regulations 1992
- Construction (Design and Management) (CDM) Regulations 2015
- Electricity at Work Regulations 1989
- Lifting Operations and Lifting Equipment Regulations (LOLER) 1998

<!-- @body.h2 -->
Site rules

<!-- @body.bullets -->
- Security and vigilance: wear your pass, challenge anyone without one, report anything unattended.
- Evacuation: when the announcement is made, stop work, leave by the nearest exit and go to the assembly point at [location]. Do not use lifts. Do not go back for belongings.
- First aid: the first aid point is at [location]. Report every injury to the exhibitor desk.
- Fire: raise the alarm at the nearest call point and leave. Fight a fire only if trained and safe.
- Closed footwear and PPE in active build zones. Keep gangways clear. No under 16s during build and breakdown. No lone working. Report accidents and near misses the same day.
