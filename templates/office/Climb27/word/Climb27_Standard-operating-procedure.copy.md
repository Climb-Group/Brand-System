Standard operating procedure

<!-- @body.title -->
Speaker [[check-in]] at the registration desk

<!-- @body.h1 -->
Purpose

<!-- @body.lead -->
Get every speaker checked in within a minute of reaching the desk, so they are in the green room on time and the stage team knows they have arrived.

<!-- @body.h1 -->
Before you start

<!-- @body.bullets -->
- The desk device is signed in and on the event's registration screen.
- Switch the desk to speaker mode: open the mode menu and choose Speaker.
- Keep the printed speaker list to hand for manual check-in.

<!-- @body.h1 -->
Check in a speaker

<!-- @body.numbers -->
1. Ask for their e-ticket and scan it.
2. Pick the session they are speaking in from the list that appears.
3. Press Check in. The screen confirms with the speaker's name and session.
4. Press Continue and hand over their pass. Point them to the green room.

<!-- @body.h2 -->
Manual check-in

<!-- @body.p -->
If the ticket will not scan, press Manual check-in, type the speaker's name, pick them from the list and check them in. If they are not on the list, escalate (below) before issuing a pass.

<!-- @body.h2 -->
Undo a mistaken check-in

<!-- @body.p -->
Open the speaker's record, press Undo check-in and confirm. Check in the right person straight after, so the stage team's list stays right.

<!-- @body.h1 -->
Warnings and when to escalate

<!-- @body.table widths=3.4,6.0,7.2 -->
| Message on screen | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Invalid badge | The ticket is not valid for this event or has already been used | Check the name on the printed list. If they are on it, check them in manually. If not, escalate. |
| No sessions | The ticket is valid but no session is linked to it | Check them in as an attendee, then escalate so the session is linked. |

<!-- @body.h2 -->
What to pass on when you escalate

<!-- @body.bullets -->
- The speaker's name and the event
- The exact message on the screen
- The session and its time
- Whether the ticket works in attendee mode

<!-- @body.callout -->
Escalate to the registration lead on the radio or at the help point. Never leave a speaker waiting at the desk: seat them nearby and bring the answer to them.
