### Who we are
In this release, "we" and "us" means The Investor Ladder Limited, trading as ClimbUK and ClimbGroup, company number 10289755, registered office Suite 3, Bignell Park Barns, Chesterton, Bicester, OX26 1TD. "The Terms" means the ClimbUK Terms and Conditions at climb-uk.com/terms (version 2026-10).

<!-- @clause -->
### What you allow
You allow us, and anyone filming for us, to film, photograph and record your image, likeness, voice, words and performance ("the Content") at this shoot or event. This includes events we attend, events run by our partners or other organisers, and our own events.

<!-- @clause -->
### How we use it
We may edit, crop, caption, subtitle and combine the Content with other material, and publish it on social media, our websites, emails, adverts, slides, screens, print, press materials, and courses and media products, to promote ClimbUK, ClimbGroup and their events and programmes.

<!-- @clause -->
### Licence
This is a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence with no time limit. You receive no fee for it. The Content belongs to us, as clause 12.5 of the Terms sets out.

<!-- @clause -->
### Partners and event organisers
When we film at an event someone else runs, or with a partner, we may share the Content with that organiser and those partners. They may post or repost it on their own channels to promote the event and their work with us. Their own use is their responsibility, as clause 12.3 of the Terms explains. The organiser's own rules for filming at their event also apply.

<!-- @clause -->
### Naming and tagging
We may name you and your organisation in captions. We tag your social accounts only when you tick the box on page 1.

<!-- @clause -->
### Fair use
We use the Content in context and keep the meaning of what you said.

<!-- @clause -->
### Your choices
If you tick the newsletter box, we add you to the ClimbUK newsletter; every email has an unsubscribe link. If you tick the Affiliate Programme box, we email you an invite to apply; clause 21 of the Terms covers the programme. {video}

<!-- @clause -->
### Changing your mind
You can withdraw permission for future use at any time by emailing marketing@climb-uk.com. We then stop new use where reasonably practicable. Content already published, licensed or archived can stay where it is, as clause 12.2 of the Terms explains.

<!-- @clause -->
### Your details
We use the details on this form to keep a record of your permission, contact you about the Content and tag you if you asked us to. We keep this form for as long as we use the Content, and email you a copy once you sign. Our Privacy Policy at climb-uk.com/privacy explains your rights. Questions about your data: dataprotection@climb-group.com.

<!-- @clause -->
### Age
You confirm you are 18 or over. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian signs below as well.

<!-- @clause -->
### The Terms
This release sits alongside the Terms, including clause 12 (photography, filming and recording), and applies at events we do not run as well as our own. Where they differ about the Content, this release applies. You can sign on paper or electronically, as clause 6 of the Terms allows. The law of England and Wales applies, as clause 31 of the Terms sets out.
