    ClimbUK brand centre

    Room to move

    A small set of steps for space, three radii, warm shadows, and tap targets that are easy to hit.

    Space

    Eight steps and a section

    Use these steps for every gap, margin and padding. The section step is the space above and below a full-width section.

    Elevation

    Warm shadows, orange glow

    Elevation on light grounds (White, Light Grey). Shadows are tinted with Dark Brown, never pure black, and stay soft and low. On dark grounds, raise a surface by moving it one step lighter (Dark Brown to Charcoal) and adding the hairline; a shadow alone disappears there.

    0

    Flat: sections, most cards on dark grounds.

    1

    Resting cards, inputs, chips.

    2

    Hovered cards, sticky bars, dropdowns.

    3

    Popovers, menus, floating ticket bar.

    4

    Modals and dialogs only.

    Hairline dark

    Edge of a raised Charcoal surface on Dark Brown.

    Glow

    Stage-light glow behind the one primary action on a dark hero or stage screen. Never on light grounds, never on more than one element.

    Tap targets

    Easy to hit

    24px is the signature compact size and meets WCAG 2.2 AA. 44px is the AAA size for buttons, menus and anything used on the move. Try both with a finger or a pointer.
