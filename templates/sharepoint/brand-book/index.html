    Before anything else

    The five rules

    1. Dark first

      The default ground is Dark Brown, with Charcoal as the second dark ground. White and Light Grey are for reading-heavy pieces: documents, long emails, forms.

    2. Orange leads, it does not flood

      One orange button or call to action per section, slide, post or screen. Around it, orange markers are welcome: an orange word, the short orange rule, a sticker, a glow.

    3. Type does the shouting

      Montserrat Bold or Black, uppercase, tight leading. One word in Climb Orange and one in italic per headline. Open Sans for everything people read.

    4. Real people, real light

      Actual attendees, speakers and stage moments, with consent. Full colour under a Dark Brown fade where text sits. No stock handshakes, empty rooms or generic skylines.

    5. Contrast is not optional

      Pick a preset and use its pairs. White on Climb Orange only at 19px bold or larger, 14pt bold in print. Check a pair.

    Graphic language

    One motif per piece

    Draw it in Climb Orange or as a tone of the ground. Motifs are decoration: keep text off busy areas and never put meaning only in a motif.

    The Ascent

    Rising steps and stacked chevrons for upward progress. Dividers, corner marks, progress bars.

    Contours

    Topographic rings, as on a climbing map, with one ring in orange. Quiet backgrounds and merch.

    Stage Light

    Soft beams and glows of orange across a dark ground. Main Stage Moments, screens, heroes.

    Connections

    Dots joined by fine lines, with one orange cluster of three. Find Your People, partners.

    Voice

    Business with a party, not a party for business

    • Confident, warm, specific and plain. Say who it is for and what they get.
    • No emojis, exclamation marks, em dashes or en dashes.
    • Never Climb on its own: always ClimbUK, ClimbGroup or Climb27.
    • No numbers, targets or guarantees. Partners get meetings with the right people.
    • Owned lines: The Festival for Business Growth, and Your business. Bigger stage.
