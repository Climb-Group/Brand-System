    The four themes

    Dark, Light and their AAA twins

    The AAA themes meet WCAG 2.2 AAA contrast on every role: 7:1 for text and 4.5:1 for large text. Pick one with the theme control at the top of the page and this whole book follows it. In the token CSS, set data-climb-theme to dark, light, aaa-dark or aaa-light on any element.
