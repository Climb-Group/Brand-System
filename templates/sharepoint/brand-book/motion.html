    A festival crowd, not a slideshow

    Things rise into place like someone stepping up onto a stage, cards spring when you touch them, logos drift past. Press play on anything below.

    Curves

    Five easings

    Motion is a confident rise into place: things arrive from below and settle, like stepping up onto a stage. Short, decisive, never bouncy. Respect prefers-reduced-motion: swap movement for a fade at duration.quick.

    Your device asks for reduced motion, so the demos below fade instead of moving. That is exactly what the brand does too.

    Rise

    cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)

    Signature curve for anything entering: fast start, long settle.

    Spring

    cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)

    Playful overshoot for hover lifts on cards, stickers and photo tiles, as on the Climb27 homepage. Use with a quick or base duration; never for page-level movement.

    Standard

    cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)

    State changes and things moving within the page.

    Exit

    cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 1, 1)

    Anything leaving: accelerate away, at about two thirds of the entrance duration.

    Linear

    linear

    Progress, marquees and stage-light drift only.

    Durations

    Short and decisive

    • Instant 100ms

      Colour and opacity feedback: hover, press.

    • Quick 160ms

      Small UI: buttons, toggles, tooltips, focus rings.

    • Base 240ms

      Cards lifting, menus opening, accordions.

    • Slow 400ms

      Panels, modals, section reveals on scroll.

    • Stage 700ms

      Hero entrances, title cards and logo reveals in video. The ceiling for any web animation.
