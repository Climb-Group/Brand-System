Our brand
Where the name comes from, what the brand stands for and the rules every piece follows.
Not a conference. A festival for business growth.
ClimbUK is business with a party, not a party for business. Real crowds under stage light, big type and an orange that glows, with a clear next step every time.

Where the name comes from
The name comes from the Investor Ladder. A business climbs the ladder to reach the next rung: an investor who fits where it is now. Investors pass businesses up and down the ladder to the rung that suits what they need.
ClimbUK is a business event, never a sport or outdoors event. Talk about climbing only when the business meaning is clear in the same line, and never use ropes, walls or mountain pictures.

Our two themes
Find Your People. The people you meet are the reason to come: fellow founders, investors, advisers and partners.
Main Stage Moments. Short, big moments: sets, announcements, awards and our own formats.

The five visual rules
Dark first. Dark Brown or Charcoal is the default ground. Light grounds are for reading-heavy pieces.
Orange leads, it does not flood. One orange button per view, with orange markers around it.
Type does the shouting. Big uppercase Montserrat, one word in orange and one in italic.
Real people, real light. Our own festival photos, never stock.
Contrast is not optional. Every text colour has a tested pair.

Writing the words?
The voice has four lines and a short list of rules. Read them before you write a headline.
Read the voice guide

Voice
How ClimbUK sounds: headline energy with the discipline of a good adviser.
Exciting and useful, in every line.
Write like it belongs on a festival poster, then make sure the reader knows exactly what to do next.

The voice in four lines
Headline energy. Short, rhythmic, confident.
Doing, not dreaming. Name the outcome and the next step.
Curated, not crowded. Sell the right people in the room, never size.
Personal, not generic. Write for one audience at a time.

Rules that always apply
No emojis, no exclamation marks and no long dashes.
Always write the full name: ClimbUK, ClimbGroup or Climb27.
No promised outcomes and no promised number of meetings.
No audience sizes or numbers we have not published.
Links say where they go, never "click here".

Words we use
agenda for the event schedule.
Find investors looking for real deal flow, so nothing reads as a promised meeting.
connect you with the right people.
fractional leaders take responsibility for a function; keep them separate from advisers.
public sector for every public body in one phrase.

Logos
Every logo for ClimbUK, ClimbGroup and Climb27, and the right one for each ground.
The right logo. Every time.
Use the master files only. Never redraw, recolour, stretch or outline a logo.

Which file to use
Reversed: the default, for Dark Brown, Charcoal and dark photos.
Full colour light: for white grounds.
Full colour grey: for Light Grey grounds.
Mono white or mono dark: one colour print and busy photos.
Tagline lockup: with The Festival for Business Growth.

Square icons
The li monogram is for favicons, app icons and anything under 64 pixels. The stacked tile, with the name on two lines, is for social avatars and larger squares.
Clear space and size
Keep the height of the large C clear on every side. Minimum width is 120 pixels on screen and 30 millimetres in print.

Orange for small text

Type
Montserrat Bold, ExtraBold and Black for headlines, uppercase with tight leading. Open Sans for everything you read.
Both are free to use under a free, open font licence.

Never do this
White text on brand orange below 19 pixels bold.
Light Grey or slate text on any orange.
Dark Brown labels on orange buttons.
Orange on orange, or a hover state lighter than the button.

Photography
Real people under real stage light: how to choose and use festival photos.
Real people. Real light.
Our own festival photos of real attendees, speakers and stage moments, used with consent. Never stock handshakes or empty rooms.

Choose a photo that
shows faces, laughter and close conversations;
has orange lanyards, branded backdrops or stage light in it;
has no readable name badges and no children;
is at least 4K, and never upscaled.

Put words on it like this
Text on a photo always sits on a solid ground or a proper fade to Dark Brown, never over a busy part of the picture.
Dense photo walls beat one lonely picture.

Drawings and devices
The hand drawn line set, the background motifs and the festival devices that carry the energy.
Make it feel like a festival.
Smooth, tablet-drawn lines, four motif families and a handful of poster devices. Use two or three per piece, not all of them at once.

Drawings
One clean line drawing per object, from tickets and microphones to rockets and ladders. Each has a soft fill shape behind it for depth.
They default to brand orange and work on every ground.

Motifs
The Ascent: rising steps, the rungs of the Investor Ladder.
Contours: rings of growth spreading out from one point.
Stage Light: soft orange beams on a dark ground.
Connections: dots joined by fine lines, people finding each other.

Festival devices

The short orange rule above a statement.
Stickers tilted a few degrees on a card corner.
Ticket cards with stub notches and a dashed tear line.
