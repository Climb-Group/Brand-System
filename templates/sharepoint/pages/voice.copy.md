## Section 1

<!-- @section layout=OneColumn emphasis=3 -->

<!-- @section.column -->
### Exciting and useful, in every line.
Write like it belongs on a festival poster, then make sure the reader knows exactly what to do next.

## Section 2

<!-- @section layout=TwoColumn emphasis=0 -->

<!-- @section.column -->
### The voice in four lines
- **Headline energy.** Short, rhythmic, confident.
- **Doing, not dreaming.** Name the outcome and the next step.
- **Curated, not crowded.** Sell the right people in the room, never size.
- **Personal, not generic.** Write for one audience at a time.

<!-- @section.column -->
### Rules that always apply
- No emojis, no exclamation marks and no long dashes.
- Always write the full name: ClimbUK, ClimbGroup or Climb27.
- No promised outcomes and no promised number of meetings.
- No audience sizes or numbers we have not published.
- Links say where they go, never "click here".

## Section 3

<!-- @section layout=OneColumn emphasis=1 -->

<!-- @section.column -->
### Words we use
- **agenda** for the event schedule.
- **Find investors looking for real deal flow**, so nothing reads as a promised meeting.
- **connect you with** the right people.
- **fractional leaders** take responsibility for a function; keep them separate from advisers.
- **public sector** for every public body in one phrase.
