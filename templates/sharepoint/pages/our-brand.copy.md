### Where the name comes from
The name comes from the Investor Ladder. A business climbs the ladder to reach the next rung: an investor who fits where it is now. Investors pass businesses up and down the ladder to the rung that suits what they need.

ClimbUK is a business event, never a sport or outdoors event. Talk about climbing only when the business meaning is clear in the same line, and never use ropes, walls or mountain pictures.

<!-- @section.column -->
### Our two themes
**Find Your People.** The people you meet are the reason to come: fellow founders, investors, advisers and partners.

**Main Stage Moments.** Short, big moments: sets, announcements, awards and our own formats.

## Section 3

<!-- @section layout=OneColumn emphasis=1 -->

<!-- @section.column -->
### The five visual rules
- **Dark first.** Dark Brown or Charcoal is the default ground. Light grounds are for reading-heavy pieces.
- **Orange leads, it does not flood.** One orange button per view, with orange markers around it.
- **Type does the shouting.** Big uppercase Montserrat, one word in orange and one in italic.
- **Real people, real light.** Our own festival photos, never stock.
- **Contrast is not optional.** Every text colour has a tested pair.
