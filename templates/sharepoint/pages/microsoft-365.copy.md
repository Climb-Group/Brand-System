## Section 1

<!-- @section layout=OneColumn emphasis=3 -->

<!-- @section.column -->
### Show up on brand.
Branded meetings, backgrounds and signatures, so every call and email looks like the festival.

## Section 2

<!-- @section layout=TwoColumn emphasis=0 -->

<!-- @section.column -->
### Meeting backgrounds
Twelve 4K backgrounds for each brand: photo, calm, festival and frosted glass. In Teams, open **Effects and avatars**, then **Video effects**, and pick one from the organisation set.

The logo sits top left and the middle stays calm for you.

<!-- @section.column -->
### Meeting themes
Branded meetings show the brand logo, image and colour in the join screen and meeting window. Your meeting policy sets the theme for you.

## Section 3

<!-- @section layout=TwoColumn emphasis=1 -->

<!-- @section.column -->
### Email signatures
Signatures are set centrally with the social buttons for LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, X and Bluesky. Do not add your own logo or banner.

<!-- @section.column -->
### Fonts and colours in your apps
Montserrat and Open Sans are set as the brand fonts across the brand centre and the templates.
