Real people under real stage light: how to choose and use festival photos.

## Section 1

<!-- @section layout=OneColumn emphasis=3 -->

<!-- @section.column -->
### Real people. Real light.
Our own festival photos of real attendees, speakers and stage moments, used with consent. Never stock handshakes or empty rooms.

## Section 2

<!-- @section layout=TwoColumn emphasis=0 -->

<!-- @section.column -->
### Choose a photo that
- shows faces, laughter and close conversations;
- has orange lanyards, branded backdrops or stage light in it;
- has no readable name badges and no children;
- is at least 4K, and never upscaled.

<!-- @section.column -->
### Put words on it like this
Text on a photo always sits on a solid ground or a proper fade to Dark Brown, never over a busy part of the picture.

Dense photo walls beat one lonely picture.
