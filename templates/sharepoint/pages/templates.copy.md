## Section 1

<!-- @section layout=OneColumn emphasis=3 -->

<!-- @section.column -->
### Start from the right file.
Never start from a blank page. Every template has the brand fonts, colours and layouts built in.

## Section 2

<!-- @section layout=ThreeColumn emphasis=0 -->

<!-- @section.column heading=h3 -->
### In Word and PowerPoint
Choose **New**, then the **ClimbUK** tab under your organisation's templates. Letterhead, proposal, report, press release, meeting notes and the 16:9 deck, for each brand.

<!-- @section.column heading=h3 -->
### In Excel
Event budget, run sheet, content calendar and partner pipeline, with the brand table styles and chart colours. Find them in the same place as the Word and PowerPoint templates.

<!-- @section.column heading=h3 -->
### For video
Titles, lower thirds, a colour look and motion loops for editing apps. The install package sets them up on Windows, macOS and Linux.
