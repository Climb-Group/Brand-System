Five colours, five tested presets and two typefaces. Pick a preset and the readable pairs come with it.

## Section 2

<!-- @section layout=ThreeColumn emphasis=0 -->

<!-- @section.column heading=h3 -->
### The palette
- **Brand orange** #E8712A
- **Dark Brown** #1E0F05
- **Charcoal** #242322
- **Light Grey** #E9E9E9
- **White** #FFFFFF

<!-- @section.column heading=h3 -->
### Orange for small text
- On white: **Orange Text** #C55308
- On Light Grey: #B04902
- On Dark Brown and Charcoal: **Orange Tint** #F2AE86 for links, brand orange for everything else

<!-- @section.column heading=h3 -->
### Type
**Montserrat** Bold, ExtraBold and Black for headlines, uppercase with tight leading. **Open Sans** for everything you read.

Both are free to use under a free, open font licence.

## Section 3

<!-- @section layout=OneColumn emphasis=1 -->

<!-- @section.column -->
### Never do this
- White text on brand orange below 19 pixels bold.
- Light Grey or slate text on any orange.
- Dark Brown labels on orange buttons.
- Orange on orange, or a hover state lighter than the button.
