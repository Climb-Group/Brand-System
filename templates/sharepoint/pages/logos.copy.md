## Section 1

<!-- @section.column -->
### The right logo. Every time.
Use the master files only. Never redraw, recolour, stretch or outline a logo.

## Section 2

<!-- @section layout=TwoColumn emphasis=0 -->

<!-- @section.column -->
### Which file to use
- **Reversed**: the default, for Dark Brown, Charcoal and dark photos.
- **Full colour light**: for white grounds.
- **Full colour grey**: for Light Grey grounds.
- **Mono white or mono dark**: one colour print and busy photos.
- **Tagline lockup**: with The Festival for Business Growth.

<!-- @section.column -->
### Square icons
The **li monogram** is for favicons, app icons and anything under 64 pixels. The **stacked tile**, with the name on two lines, is for social avatars and larger squares.

#### Clear space and size

Keep the height of the large C clear on every side. Minimum width is 120 pixels on screen and 30 millimetres in print.
