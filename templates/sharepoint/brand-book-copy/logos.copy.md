### Give it room
Clear space: the height of the capital C on every side. Turn on Show clear space in the lab to see it.

Minimum width: 120px on screen, 30mm in print. Below that, use the monogram.

Square icons: the li monogram for favicons, app icons and anything under 64px; the stacked tile for social avatars and larger squares.

Inks: Climb Orange with Charcoal on light grounds, Climb Orange with White on dark grounds, the deeper orange on Light Grey.

Never stretch, rotate, outline, add effects, or set the logo on a busy part of a photo. No dates or places are baked into any logo.
