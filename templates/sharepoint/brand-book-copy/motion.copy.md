### Five easings
Motion is a confident rise into place: things arrive from below and settle, like stepping up onto a stage. Short, decisive, never bouncy. Respect prefers-reduced-motion: swap movement for a fade at duration.quick.

Your device asks for reduced motion, so the demos below fade instead of moving. That is exactly what the brand does too.

<!-- @rise -->
### Rise
cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)

Signature curve for anything entering: fast start, long settle.

<!-- @spring -->
### Spring
cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)

Playful overshoot for hover lifts on cards, stickers and photo tiles, as on the Climb27 homepage. Use with a quick or base duration; never for page-level movement.

Play spring
