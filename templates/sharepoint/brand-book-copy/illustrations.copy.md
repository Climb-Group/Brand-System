<!-- @one-hero-a-few -->
### One hero, a few ghosts
One hero drawing tied to the message, with two or three ghosted copies behind it: the same drawing, rotated, at 12 to 30 percent opacity.

Never behind or touching the type.

Orange ink on dark grounds; Charcoal or Dark Brown ink on light grounds; White or Dark Brown ink on Climb Orange.

Drawings are decoration. Never put meaning only in a drawing: say it in words too.

No venue, building or place appears in the set.
