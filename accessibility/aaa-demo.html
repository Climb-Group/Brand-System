E9 · 1.4.8 Visual presentation

Line spacing and line length

AAA asks for body text with line spacing of at least 1.5, paragraph gaps at least 1.5 times that, lines under 80 characters, no justified text and no tiny print. Headlines are exempt and keep their tight poster look.

E12 · 2.4.12 Focus not obscured

Nothing hides under the sticky header

When someone tabs to a link or follows a jump link, the browser scrolls it to the very top of the window, which is right where the sticky header sits. The fix tells the browser to stop that far below the top. It changes nothing for mouse users scrolling normally.
