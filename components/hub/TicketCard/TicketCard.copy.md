## Usage notes

<!-- @readme.text -->
The ticket card is the thing people pick up: a glass card with stub notches, a sticker and one button.

<!-- @readme.item -->
`climb-ticket` on dark grounds; add `climb-ticket-hl` for the one ticket to pick (orange edge and glow).

<!-- @readme.item -->
On Climb Orange it turns Dark Brown.

<!-- @readme.item -->
Kicker, name, three short benefits, one button. Prices go on offer cards.

<!-- @readme.item -->
`climb-levels` lists packages with a note on the right (passes, sizes).
