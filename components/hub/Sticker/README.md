# Sticker

Stickers are the festival's stamps: one or two words, tilted, pinned to the corner of a photo or a ticket.

- `climb-sticker` is Climb Orange with Dark Brown text; `climb-sticker-dark` is Dark Brown with orange text.
- `climb-sticker-pin` pins it to the top corner of its card.
- One sticker per card. Words only, never a sentence.
