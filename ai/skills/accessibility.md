# ClimbUK accessibility

Use this skill whenever you produce or review anything people read, watch or use for ClimbUK, ClimbGroup or Climb27, and whenever someone asks whether something is accessible.

The baseline is WCAG 2.2 AA. The brand adopts AAA wherever it is small and easy, and every token set has an AAA variant (AAA Dark and AAA Light themes).

## Colour and contrast

- Normal text needs 4.5:1 (AAA 7:1). Large text (24px, or 19px bold) needs 3:1 (AAA 4.5:1). Icons, borders and focus rings need 3:1.
- Call the check colours tool for any pair you are not taking straight from a preset, and report the ratio and verdict.
- White on Climb Orange is only for 19px bold or larger. White labels stay on orange buttons: Dark Brown labels on orange were rejected.
- Small orange text uses Orange Tint #F2AE86 on dark grounds, Deep Orange #8D4419 on White and #7A3B16 on Light Grey.
- Never use colour alone to carry meaning (sold out, errors, states): say it in words too.

## Words

- Links say where they go: never "click here", "read more" or "learn more".
- Spell out abbreviations on first use. Aim for reading grade 9 or lower. Long pages open with a two-line summary.
- Every image that carries meaning has alt text that says what it shows and why it is there; decorative images have empty alt text.
- Every section has a heading, visually hidden if the design shows none.

## Interaction and media

- Touch targets 44px; 24px only for dense rows where AA applies.
- Visible focus on everything interactive, never hidden behind a sticky header.
- Respect reduced motion: no hover lifts, springs or scroll rises when it is on. Web animation never runs longer than 700ms.
- Recorded video has captions and a transcript; speech sits at least 20 dB above music, with a dialogue-only track where the system supports it.
- Pop-ups and banners can be dismissed and postponed.

Call the guide tool with the topic "accessibility" for the full accessibility guidance.
