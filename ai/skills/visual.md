# ClimbUK visual style

Use this skill whenever you design, lay out, recolour, describe or review anything made to be seen for ClimbUK, ClimbGroup or Climb27: web sections, emails, social graphics, slides, documents, video, wallpapers, call backgrounds, print, merch, signage and stage screens. Pair it with the voice skill for any words on the piece.

A festival poster with a buy button on it: full festival, fun, punchy, exciting. Never a corporate conference look.

## The five rules

1. Dark first. Default ground is Dark Brown #1E0F05 or Charcoal #242322. White and Light Grey are for reading-heavy pieces: documents, long emails, forms.
2. Orange leads, it does not flood. One orange button or call to action per section, slide, post or screen, with orange markers around it (an orange word, the short orange rule, a sticker, a glow). Full orange grounds are for the big moments.
3. Type does the shouting. Montserrat Bold or Black, uppercase for display lines, tight leading, one word in Climb Orange and one in italic per headline. Open Sans for reading. Never more than two sizes of display type on one view.
4. Real people, real light. Photos of real attendees and stage moments, with consent. No stock handshakes, empty rooms or generic skylines.
5. Contrast is not optional. Use a preset's pairs. White on Climb Orange only at 19px bold or larger (14pt bold in print).

## Colours

Only use colours from the brand tokens. Never type a hex value you have not got from the palette or tokens tool. #EC752A is retired: Climb Orange is #E8712A. Before you recommend a colour pair that is not in a preset, call the check colours tool and report its ratio.

Call the palette tool for the five presets (White, Light Grey, Dark Brown, Climb Orange, Charcoal) and the Dark, Light, AAA Dark and AAA Light themes. Call the tokens tool for type sizes, spacing, radii, shadows, motion and print colours (Pantone, CMYK, RAL).

## Graphic language

One motif family per piece: The Ascent (rising steps and chevrons), Contours (topographic lines), Stage Light (orange beams and glow), Connections (dots and fine lines), or the Highlight bar (an orange bar behind one word). Motifs are decoration: never carry meaning only in a motif, and keep text off busy pattern areas.

Festival devices, two or three per piece: the orange word and the italic word, the short orange rule, stickers tilted -3 to -4 degrees, ticket cards with stub notches, photo cards and walls, lineups and marquees, cut edges and glow, photo fades to Dark Brown.

## Making an asset

1. Call the surface tool for size, safe zone, format and preset.
2. Lay out: ground or photo, one display line, one or two devices or one motif, the logo in its corner (call the logo tool), one call to action if the surface has one.
3. Write the words with the voice skill and the check copy tool.
4. Check contrast, safe zone, logo clear space, and the file name: <brand>_<surface>_<variant>_<size>.<ext>.
5. Default resolution is 4K for video, photo and screens. Never upscale.

For motion, UI states, photography, video, print and signatures, call the guide tool with that topic.
