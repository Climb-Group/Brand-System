# ClimbUK campaigns and channels

Use this skill when you plan or write a campaign, social post, email, newsletter, partner share kit, ad or event app message for ClimbUK, ClimbGroup or Climb27.

## Before you write

1. Name the audience. Call the audience tool: it returns what they need, the tone note, their page and the headline the site uses. Where it says "Ask before writing", ask the person first.
2. Name the channel. Call the channel tool: it returns the channel's job, its shape (lengths and structure) and the call to action style.
3. If the piece has an image or video, call the surface tool for its size, safe zone and preset, and follow the visual style skill.

## Rules

- Audience first: marketing is led by audience, not by event. People who have been before come first in every campaign.
- Attendee pages and partner pages are separate: do not sell a stand and a ticket in the same piece.
- Social builds reach and free sign-ups; sales come from search, email, partners and recommendations. Write each channel for its job.
- Email: subject 3 to 7 words, no clickbait; the preheader completes it; one message and one primary button per email.
- Every link to an audience page carries UTM tags: utm_source (the platform), utm_medium (social, email, partner, paid), utm_campaign=climb27, utm_content (the post or asset name). Never post a bare audience-page link.
- Partner share kits: three ready posts per partner (short, medium, long), in the partner's own voice ("We are at Climb27").
- Ticket messages name the release, its price and its state ("Open now", "Next", "Final"). Team tickets are 25% off: sell why the team should come, then the offer.

Write every piece with the voice skill and pass it through the check copy tool before you offer it. When you hand over files (a share kit, an email, a set of posts), give their words as a copy file too: one Markdown file with each part marked, such as `<!-- @post.short -->`, so the team can review and change the words without opening the designs.
