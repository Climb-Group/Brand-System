# ClimbUK logos

Use this skill when someone needs a logo for ClimbUK, ClimbGroup or Climb27, asks which logo file to use, or when you place a logo on anything.

Always call the logo tool with the brand, the ground (dark, white, light grey, orange or photo), the width in pixels if known, and the use (for example favicon, app icon, social avatar, email header, slide). Give the person the variant, the file names it returns and the reasons.

## Rules

- Use the master files only. Never recolour, outline, stretch, rotate or redraw the logo, set it in a font, or add effects to it.
- The logo tool returns the clear space and minimum sizes for wordmarks, tagline lockups, the square tile and the monogram. Quote them from the tool, not from memory.
- Square icons: the l! monogram for favicons, app icons and anything under 64px; the stacked tile for social avatars and squares of 64px or more.
- Wordmark colours: Climb Orange with Charcoal on White; Climb Orange with White on dark grounds; the grey version on Light Grey; mono white on Climb Orange.
- On a photo, put the logo on a calm area, or darken the photo behind it first.
- The tagline lockup reads "The Festival for Business Growth" with no dates or places. Do not lock the logo up with any other mark or slogan.
- ClimbUK is the brand on the poster. ClimbGroup is the organisation: footers, legal lines, corporate decks. On event pieces the edition mark (Climb27) leads, with ClimbUK as the endorsement.
- Variants: full-colour-light, full-colour-grey, reversed-dark (the default), mono-dark, mono-white, tagline-lockup, tagline-lockup-reversed, icon (stacked tile), icon-monogram (l!). Each comes as SVG and as PNG at 512, 1024 and 2048 pixels, in the brand downloads. Use the SVG wherever the software accepts it.
