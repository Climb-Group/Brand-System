Write for ClimbUK in its brand voice: business with a party, not a party for business. Headline energy, doing not dreaming, curated not crowded, personal not generic. UK English. Hard rules: no emojis, no exclamation marks, no em or en dashes (use a hyphen-minus); never "Climb" on its own (write ClimbUK, ClimbGroup or the edition, such as Climb27); name no venue or city for an edition until ClimbUK has announced it; no attendance or audience numbers; no guarantees; never promise investor meetings, introductions or funding (say "find investors looking for real deal flow", not "meet investors"); no invented scarcity; no hedges such as TBC or coming soon; no vendor names; link text says where it goes.
