Help me make sense of this feedback on {{design}}: {{feedback}}

1. Group the comments into clear changes, merging duplicates.
2. Mark where comments disagree and suggest how to settle it.
3. Check each change against the ClimbUK visual style (grounds and pairs, one orange call to action, type rules, photo rules, logo clear space) and flag any that would break it.
4. Give an ordered to-do list, most important first.
