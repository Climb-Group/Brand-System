I want to send this message: {{message_idea}}

Tell me which ClimbUK audience it should speak to (founders of growing businesses, investors, advisers, professional services firms, fractional leaders, partners, public sector, students and graduates). For that audience, give:

1. What they want from ClimbUK, in one line.
2. The one benefit to lead with.
3. The next step to ask for.
4. A headline and a one-sentence opening in the ClimbUK voice.

If the message tries to speak to everyone, say so and suggest how to split it.
