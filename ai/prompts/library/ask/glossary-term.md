Explain the term "{{term}}" for the ClimbUK website glossary.

Give: the term as it should be written, an anchor in the form term-<slug>, and one or two plain sentences (grade 9 reading level or lower) that a newcomer to growing a business would understand. Write it in the ClimbUK voice: useful, direct, no jargon inside the definition. If the term has a common abbreviation, spell it out first and give the short form in brackets.
