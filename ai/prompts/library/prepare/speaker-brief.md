Prepare a briefing for {{speaker}} speaking on {{session}}.

Include: the session format, length and time; who is in the room and what they want to take away; the run of the session minute by minute; tech and slides (ClimbUK slide template, 16:9, captions); accessibility asks (describe visuals out loud, plain words); brand notes (no promises of investment or meetings, say ClimbUK not Climb); arrival and contacts as placeholders.

Keep it to one page, friendly and clear. Name no venue if the venue is not yet announced.
