Write it in the ClimbUK voice: warm, direct, no hedging and no defensive wording. Say the decision or news in the first two sentences, give the reason in one sentence, then what happens next or what we can offer instead. No apology loops, no blame. Give me two versions: one short (under 80 words) and one fuller (under 180 words). Do not send anything.
