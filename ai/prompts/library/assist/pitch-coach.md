Coach me on my ClimbUK partner pitch to {{company}}. Here is my pitch or notes: {{pitch}}

1. Play a sceptical marketing director at {{company}}. Ask me the five hardest questions they would ask, one at a time, and wait for my answer each time.
2. After each answer, tell me what landed, what did not, and a sharper way to say it in the ClimbUK voice.
3. At the end, give me a 30-second version of the pitch and the one question I should ask them.

Keep it honest: partners are sold meetings with the right people and moments on the day, never audience numbers, guaranteed leads or reports.
