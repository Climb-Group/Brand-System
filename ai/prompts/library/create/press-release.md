Write a press release for ClimbUK announcing {{news}}. Facts I can confirm: {{facts}}

Lead on what the news means for the wider public, not only for the people at the event. Name who outside the room benefits and how: people who could not otherwise start a business, young people, jobs, communities, the region, customers, public safety. If the effect is too narrow, the press will not print it.

Structure: a headline and a one-sentence summary that carry the public angle. A first paragraph that sets out the public problem. Then what ClimbUK or the partner does about it, in facts they publish, with who, what and when. Then one supporting paragraph. Then an empty quote slot, [Quote from a named, consenting person], with a one-line brief that steers the speaker to the wider impact. Then a closing line with one next step. Then Notes to editors with the press contact for every organisation in the story: ClimbUK marketing@climb-uk.com, ClimbGroup marketing@climb-group.com, and each partner's press contact.

Any figure comes from an official or primary source, named in the same sentence. Use only the facts given. Never invent quotes, names, numbers or venues. No superlatives, no comparisons with other events. Write it so a journalist can lift any paragraph on its own.
