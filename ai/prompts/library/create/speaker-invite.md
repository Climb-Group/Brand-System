Write an invitation from ClimbUK to {{speaker}} to speak about {{topic}}.

1. Open with why them, using something specific they have done: {{why_them}}.
2. Say what we are asking: format (talk, panel, roundtable or workshop), length and the audience in the room.
3. Say what they get: the people they will find, the stage moment, how we promote their session.
4. One next step: a reply or a short call.

Keep it under 180 words, warm and direct. Do not promise audience numbers, fees or coverage I have not stated.
