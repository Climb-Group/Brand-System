Check this copy against the ClimbUK brand voice:

{{copy}}

1. List every hard-rule break (dashes, exclamation marks, emojis, "Climb" on its own, venue hints, numbers, guarantees, promised meetings, invented scarcity, hedges, vendor names, vague link text) with the exact words and the fix.
2. Score the copy from 1 to 5 on each of the four voice lines: headline energy, doing not dreaming, curated not crowded, personal not generic. One sentence of evidence per score.
3. Give a corrected version that keeps the meaning and length.
4. Say who the copy is for. If it could be about any event, say so and suggest the specific detail that fixes it.
