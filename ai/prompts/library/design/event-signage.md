Plan the event signage for {{area}} at a ClimbUK edition.

1. List every sign needed (wayfinding, room or stage names, timings, stand numbers, banners, QR points), with size and where it hangs.
2. For each: the words in the ClimbUK voice, the reading distance and the minimum letter height for it.
3. Ground and colour pairs from the brand presets, with high contrast for wayfinding.
4. Icons from the ClimbUK illustration set where they help.
5. Accessible routes and step-free information on every wayfinding sign.

Name no venue in anything that will be shared before the venue is announced.
