Plan a ClimbUK social graphic for {{channel}} about {{message}}.

Give me:
1. The size and safe zone for that channel.
2. The ground preset (Dark Brown by default) and the colour pairs to use.
3. The headline in uppercase with the orange word and the italic word marked, plus a short line under it. Never more than two sizes of display type.
4. One motif family (Ascent, Contours, Stage Light, Connections or the highlight bar) and where it sits.
5. Photo direction: real ClimbUK attendees under stage light, text only on a solid ground or a fade that covers at most a third, nothing over faces.
6. Logo version and placement, with clear space of a large C on every edge.
7. Alt text for the finished graphic.

Do not invent colour values or logo files: name them from the brand pack.
