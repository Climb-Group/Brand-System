1. Where things stand, in three sentences.
2. Decisions made and by whom, with dates.
3. Open questions and anything they are waiting on from us.
4. Upcoming meetings or deadlines.
5. The next thing I should do.

Link each point to its source. Work only from what you find; if something is unclear, say so instead of guessing.
