Build a ClimbUK content calendar for {{period}}, leading up to {{milestone}}.

As a table: date, channel, audience, theme, headline idea in the ClimbUK voice, format (post, video, email, story), call to action and owner. Balance the audiences across the weeks. Build up energy towards the milestone, with ticket release moments named by release and state. Leave room for reacting to news. Keep posting times sensible for UK audiences.
