# Organisation logos

The logos of the partners, exhibitors and organisations shown in the Who was there pack. They are the same files the public Who was there page on the ClimbUK website shows, resized to 640px on the long edge to keep the pack small. Each file is listed in `climb27_who-was-there.copy.md` with the organisation's name.

Each logo belongs to its organisation and is used to show who took part in Climb25 and Climb26. Do not use them for anything else, and remove a logo when its organisation asks.

## Company icons for speakers and sessions

The website does not carry these, so the pack uses each company's square profile icon from its public company profile, saved at 400px: `bbc.png`, `channel-4.png`, `dazn.png`, `infinity-blue-group.png`, `lego.png`, `linkedin.png`, `lovable.png`, `microsoft.png`, `octopus-investments.png`, `sky-sports.png`, `sony.png`, `tiktok.png`, `walmart-marketplace.png`, `lloyds-banking-group.png`, `santander-uk.png`, `pwc.png`, `snowflake.png` and `squire-patton-boggs.png`. Swap a file for the company's own wordmark from its brand or press page when one is to hand; keep the file name.
