## The rules

<!-- @rule -->
### Logo
- **Master files only.** Never redraw, recolour, stretch or add effects.
- **Reversed on dark by default.** Full colour on White, grey on Light Grey, mono white on orange.
- **Clear space:** the height of the C in Climb. **Minimum:** 120px or 30mm, then the li monogram.

<!-- @rule -->
### Colour
- **Dark first:** Charcoal is the ground. Light grounds are for long reading.
- **Orange leads, never floods:** one orange call to action per view.
- **White on orange** only at 19px bold or 14pt bold and up. Small orange text: Orange Tint on dark, Deep Orange on white.

<!-- @rule -->
### Type
- **Montserrat** headings: uppercase, tight, 3 to 8 words. **Open Sans** for reading.
- **One word orange, one word italic** in every display line.
- **Nothing under 13px** on screen or 9pt in print.

<!-- @rule.type-sample -->
<!-- note: The sample headline printed under the Type rule. -->
ICONIC *MOMENTS*. REAL ADVICE. NO [[FLUFF]].

<!-- @rule quotes=curly example=avoid -->
### Voice
- **Headline energy. Doing, not dreaming. Curated, not crowded. Personal, not generic.**
- **No emojis, exclamation marks or dashes.** Full names only, never "Climb" alone.
- **No unpublished numbers, no guarantees, no hedges.** Scarcity only when true.
- **Real, named people** in quotes. Links say where they go.

<!-- @rule -->
### Photography and graphics
- **Real people, real light,** warm and full colour, with consent. Never stock.
- **Words sit on a Dark Brown fade,** never on a busy part of a photo.
- **One motif family** and two or three festival devices per piece.

<!-- @rule -->
### Everyone in the room
- **WCAG 2.2 AA everywhere,** AAA where it is easy.
- **44px targets,** a visible focus ring, colour never the only signal.
- **Plain words,** alt text that says what is happening, captions on video.
- **4K by default,** never upscaled. Print PDF/X-4 with 3mm bleed.
