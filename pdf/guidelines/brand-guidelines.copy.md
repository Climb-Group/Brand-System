A FESTIVAL *POSTER*\

<!-- @five-rules.rule -->
### Dark first
Dark Brown or Charcoal is the default ground. Light grounds are for long reading: documents, long emails and forms.

One orange call to action per view. Around it, orange markers: a word, a rule, a sticker, a glow.

Pick a ground and use its colour pairs. White on Climb Orange only at 19px bold or larger.

## 07 Motion and digital

<!-- @motion.eyebrow -->
Motion

<!-- @motion.headline -->
A FESTIVAL *CROWD*,\
NOT A [[SLIDESHOW]].

<!-- @motion.points -->
- **Rise, then settle.** Entrances travel up and slow down.
- **One thing leads.** Everything else is still or follows in a short stagger.
- **Light and crowds move, type holds.**
- **Touch gets a spring:** one small, joyful overshoot.
- **Always optional:** reduced motion swaps movement for a quick fade.

## 08 Voice

<!-- @voice-lines.eyebrow -->
The voice in four lines

<!-- @voice-lines.headline -->
EXCITING *AND* [[USEFUL]].

<!-- @voice-lines.line -->
### Headline energy
Write like it belongs on a billboard, a stage screen or a festival poster. Short, rhythmic, confident.

<!-- @voice-lines.line -->
### Doing, not dreaming
Move the reader from inspiration to action. Name the outcome and the next step.

<!-- @voice-lines.line -->
### Curated, not crowded
Sell the right people in the room and the quality of the conversations. Never sell size.

<!-- @voice-lines.line -->
### Personal, not generic
Write every piece for one audience. If it could be about any event, rewrite it.

<!-- @voice-lines.marquee -->
<!-- note: A faint outline line across the bottom of the page. -->
Find your people. Then do business with them. Iconic moments. Real advice. No fluff.

<!-- @voice-rules.eyebrow -->
The rules

<!-- @voice-rules.headline -->
LET THE *DESIGN* DO THE [[SHOUTING]].

<!-- note: Each crossed-out sign: the glyph setting is what prints inside the circle (dash prints the long dash the rule bans), then the rule and why. -->

<!-- @voice-rules.sign glyph=":)" -->
### No emojis
Let design and photography carry the energy.

<!-- @voice-rules.sign glyph="!" -->
### No exclamation marks
Full stops do it with more confidence.

<!-- @voice-rules.sign glyph=dash -->
### No dashes
Use full stops, commas and colons.

<!-- @voice-rules.points quotes=curly example=avoid -->
- **Full names.** ClimbUK, ClimbGroup or the edition, Climb27. Never "Climb" on its own.
- **No numbers we have not published.** No audience sizes or counts of partners or meetings.
- **No promises we cannot keep.** No guarantees, no promised meetings, introductions or funding.
- **Scarcity only when it is true.** Name the release, its price and its state.
- **No hedges.** No TBC, coming soon or we hope. Say what happens, or leave it out.
- **Real people only.** Quotes come from named people who agreed to them.
- **No vendor names.** Say what the reader can do, not what powers it.
