USE CLIMBGROUP ON

<!-- @where.yes -->

## Documents and decks

<!-- @documents.eyebrow -->
Documents and decks

<!-- @documents.headline -->
A *POSTER* ON THE FRONT. A [[PAGE]] INSIDE.

<!-- @documents.points -->
- **Covers are festival:** a real photo, a Dark Brown fade, the angled orange slab and a big uppercase title.
- **Inside pages are for reading:** White grounds, Body Slate text, Charcoal headings, the short orange rule.
- **Decks:** dark title and section slides, light slides for detail. One idea per slide.
- **Legal lines** carry the ClimbGroup name and registered details in small type, 13px or 9pt at least.

## Email signatures

<!-- @signature.eyebrow -->
Email signatures

<!-- @signature.headline -->
EVERY EMAIL IS A [[FLYER]].

<!-- @signature.points -->
- Live text for everything people read, so it works with images off and with screen readers.
- One campaign banner, button or sticker, never more. Swap it with each campaign.
- Social icons are buttons at 24px, with a 44px version for easier tapping.
- Works on light and dark mail themes.
