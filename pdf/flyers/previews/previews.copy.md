# Climb27 flyer contact sheets

<!-- note: The sheets show the flyers, whose words live in pdf/flyers/copy/. Each section here is the alt text for one sheet. -->

<!-- @alt file=climb27_flyer_segments_fronts.jpg -->
The fronts of the eight Climb27 flyers for each audience, in two rows: a Climb26 photo at the top of each, an orange panel with the audience and headline, and a dark band at the foot with the next step and the website.

<!-- @alt file=climb27_flyer_segments_backs.jpg -->
The backs of the eight Climb27 flyers for each audience, in two rows: five reasons with an icon each, a strip of three photos and an orange band at the foot with a QR code.

<!-- @alt file=climb27_flyer_what-is-climbuk.jpg -->
The front and back of the flyer for people new to ClimbUK. The front shows the Climb26 crew in orange T-shirts with the headline The Festival for Business Growth; the back lists what ClimbUK is, who comes, what happens, your next step and where you fit, with three photos and a QR code.

<!-- @alt file=climb27_flyer_general-variants_fronts.jpg -->
The fronts of the four Climb27 flyers for people new to ClimbUK side by side, each with its own photo, headline and sticker.

<!-- @alt file=climb27_flyer_general-variants_backs.jpg -->
The backs of the four Climb27 flyers for people new to ClimbUK side by side: a headline, five reasons with an icon each, photos and an orange band with a QR code.
