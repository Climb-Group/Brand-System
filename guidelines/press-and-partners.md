# Press, publishers and partners

For journalists, publishers, partners, sponsors, speakers and anyone putting ClimbUK next to their own name. Words follow the Voice section.

## Naming

- **ClimbUK** is the brand and the festival. **Climb27** is this year's edition (Climb26 was last year's). **ClimbGroup** is the company name, for legal lines and contracts; do not explain how it relates to ClimbUK in your copy.
- Never "Climb" on its own, never "Climb UK" with a space, never "CLIMBUK" in body text. Uppercase is a display style for headlines, not a spelling.
- The descriptor is "the festival for business growth". As a tagline, capitalise it: "The Festival for Business Growth".
- Dates: 30 June and 1 July 2027.
- Programme names: Investor Speed Networking, Busk Your Business, the ClimbAwards, ClimbHealth, the ClimbGroup Event App.

## Boilerplate

Use as written. Shorten only by cutting whole sentences.

**Short (about 50 words)**

> ClimbUK is the festival for business growth. It brings founders, investors, advisers, universities and councils together for two days of main stage moments, close conversations and after parties, so growing businesses meet the people who can help them grow. Climb27 takes place on 30 June and 1 July 2027.

**Long (about 100 words)**

> ClimbUK is the festival for business growth: two days where founders, investors, advisers, universities and councils meet, learn and do business together. It grew out of events for founders and investors that have run since 2016, and it is built around what growing businesses need next: the right introductions, honest advice and a stage for their story. The festival mixes main stage moments with roundtables, Investor Speed Networking, the ClimbHealth stage, the ClimbAwards and the after parties. Climb27 takes place on 30 June and 1 July 2027.

**One line:** ClimbUK, the festival for business growth. Programme and tickets at climb-uk.com

## Logos for press and publishers

- Download the masters from the media kit, never from a search engine. SVG for print and web, PNG at 2048px for anything that cannot take SVG.
- Reversed (White and Climb Orange) on dark or photo backgrounds, full colour on white, mono when the publication prints in one colour.
- Clear space is the height of the large "C" in Climb on every side, around the whole logo. Minimum width 120px on screen, 30mm in print.
- Do not recolour, outline, stretch, add a shadow, or set the logo on a busy part of a photo.

## Photos and video for press

- Use photos from the ClimbUK media library with the credit line given there ("Photo: ClimbUK" unless a photographer is named).
- Crop freely, but do not alter what happened or who was there. No filters that change the colours of the brand.
- Video clips may be cut for length with the source credited. Do not re-caption speakers.

## Quotes and figures

- Quotes come from named, real people who agreed to them. ClimbUK supplies spokesperson quotes on request.
- Use only the figures ClimbUK publishes for an edition, and cite them to that edition ("at Climb26"). Do not project or round them up.

## Co-branding with partners

- **Lockup:** the partner logo and the ClimbUK logo side by side at equal optical height, separated by a 1px rule in the ground's quiet line colour, with clear space of the "C" height between each logo and the rule. ClimbUK sits on the right on partner-led pieces and on the left on ClimbUK-led pieces.
- **Lines partners may use:** "In partnership with ClimbUK", "Official partner of Climb27", "Headline partner of Climb27" (headline partners only), "Speaking at Climb27", "Exhibiting at Climb27".
- **On ClimbUK pieces,** partner logos appear in one tone (White on dark, Charcoal or grey on light) so no brand shouts over another; headline partners may appear in full colour in their own slot.
- **Never:** merge the logos into one mark, put ClimbUK colours on a partner logo, imply ClimbUK endorses a product, or promise attendees anything ClimbUK has not.
- Partners sell their presence with "meetings with the right people", never a number of meetings, a percentage or an audience size.

## Sales brochures

- **Format:** 16:9 pages, sent as a PDF or shown on screen in a call.
- **Look:** photo-led, like the Climb26 exhibitor brochure. Big uppercase lines in orange label boxes, stands shown as renders with a round size badge, prices in a solid box, and real quotes from people who came, with their name and company.
- **Partnerships are options.** Every partnership is built around what the partner wants. Show the level (Headline Partner or Partner), the things a partner can own and the extras they can add, then invite a call. Never fixed tiers, long text pages or figures.
- **Every page earns its place:** one idea per page, one photo wall or render, and a clear next step on the last page.
- **Vary the grounds.** Dark Brown on two pages at most, and never two pages in a row on the same colour.
- **At least 20 testimonials** in each document, real and attributed, and never the same quote twice in one document.
- **Plenty of exhibitors.** Fill the photo walls with exhibitors and their stands, and show a real photo of each stand type.
- **A clear closing page.** The QR code sits centred in its own white card, beside the headline, the button and the contact details.
- **Reading text never sits on orange.** Pages with body copy, lists or quotes use White, Light Grey, Charcoal or Dark Brown. Keep full orange pages for the cover moment and the closing call to action, where only big white lines sit on the orange.
- **Cards stand out from their page.** On a dark page a card is one step lighter (Charcoal on Dark Brown) with a hairline; never a card the same colour as the page.
- **Cards in a row line up.** Every card in a row has a short description, the same height and its price on one shared line at the bottom.
- **Lists are read at a glance.** List text is at least 24px on a 1920px page, sized to match its arrow or icon, with clear space between the list and any quote box below it.
- **Each stand type gets its own photos.** The starter pod is shown with a plain render, not another company's custom pods (those photos suit the expo stand). Pod photos go on the pod page and nowhere else.
- **The marketplace is for makers and new businesses.** Consultants and service firms belong in the starter pods, not at a marketplace table. Social enterprises and community organisations get a marketplace option of their own.
- **The marketplace page shows the festival.** Six or seven busy festival photos (packed rooms, arrivals, networking), not more stand shots.
- **Closing page contact lines** sit centred under the button, in white on a solid panel, with the web address written in full: www.climb-uk.com/partnerships. One logo only: Climb27 (or its tagline lockup), never side by side with ClimbUK.
