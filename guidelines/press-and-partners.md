# Press, publishers and partners

For journalists, publishers, partners, sponsors, speakers and anyone putting ClimbUK next to their own name. Words follow the Voice section.

## Naming

- **ClimbUK** is the brand and the festival. **Climb27** is this year's edition (Climb26 was last year's). **ClimbGroup** is the company name, for legal lines and contracts; do not explain how it relates to ClimbUK in your copy.
- Never "Climb" on its own, never "Climb UK" with a space, never "CLIMBUK" in body text. Uppercase is a display style for headlines, not a spelling.
- The descriptor is "the festival for business growth". As a tagline, capitalise it: "The Festival for Business Growth".
- Dates: 30 June and 1 July 2027.
- Programme names: Investor Speed Networking, Busk Your Business, the ClimbAwards, ClimbHealth, the ClimbGroup Event App.

## Boilerplate

Use as written. Shorten only by cutting whole sentences.

**Short (about 50 words)**

> ClimbUK is the festival for business growth. It brings founders, investors, advisers, universities and councils together for two days of main stage moments, close conversations and after parties, so growing businesses meet the people who can help them grow. Climb27 takes place on 30 June and 1 July 2027.

**Long (about 100 words)**

> ClimbUK is the festival for business growth: two days where founders, investors, advisers, universities and councils meet, learn and do business together. It grew out of events for founders and investors that have run since 2016, and it is built around what growing businesses need next: the right introductions, honest advice and a stage for their story. The festival mixes main stage moments with roundtables, Investor Speed Networking, the ClimbHealth stage, the ClimbAwards and the after parties. Climb27 takes place on 30 June and 1 July 2027.

**One line:** ClimbUK, the festival for business growth. Programme and tickets at climb-uk.com

## Logos for press and publishers

- Download the masters from the media kit, never from a search engine. SVG for print and web, PNG at 2048px for anything that cannot take SVG.
- Reversed (White and Climb Orange) on dark or photo backgrounds, full colour on white, mono when the publication prints in one colour.
- Clear space is the height of the large "C" in Climb on every side, around the whole logo. Minimum width 120px on screen, 30mm in print.
- Do not recolour, outline, stretch, add a shadow, or set the logo on a busy part of a photo.

## Photos and video for press

- Use photos from the ClimbUK media library with the credit line given there ("Photo: ClimbUK" unless a photographer is named).
- Crop freely, but do not alter what happened or who was there. No filters that change the colours of the brand.
- Video clips may be cut for length with the source credited. Do not re-caption speakers.

## Quotes and figures

- Quotes come from named, real people who agreed to them. ClimbUK supplies spokesperson quotes on request.
- Use only the figures ClimbUK publishes for an edition, and cite them to that edition ("at Climb26"). Do not project or round them up.

## Co-branding with partners

- **Lockup:** the partner logo and the ClimbUK logo side by side at equal optical height, separated by a 1px rule in the ground's quiet line colour, with clear space of the "C" height between each logo and the rule. ClimbUK sits on the right on partner-led pieces and on the left on ClimbUK-led pieces.
- **Lines partners may use:** "In partnership with ClimbUK", "Official partner of Climb27", "Headline partner of Climb27" (headline partners only), "Speaking at Climb27", "Exhibiting at Climb27".
- **On ClimbUK pieces,** partner logos appear in one tone (White on dark, Charcoal or grey on light) so no brand shouts over another; headline partners may appear in full colour in their own slot.
- **Never:** merge the logos into one mark, put ClimbUK colours on a partner logo, imply ClimbUK endorses a product, or promise attendees anything ClimbUK has not.
- Partners sell their presence with "meetings with the right people", never a number of meetings or an audience size for the event. Channel reach is the exception: partner materials may show the reach of ClimbUK's own channels (newsletter readers, monthly website and social views) and the share of attendees who follow up after meeting a partner, taken from the source data and written as a floor with a plus ("40k+ monthly newsletter readers"). Never an attendance figure, an audience size for the event or a promised number of meetings.

## What a partnership includes

Use this list when you describe partnerships. Pick the lines that suit the piece; there is no need to print them all.

**Included with every partnership:**

- The partner's logo on the ClimbUK website, with links to their website.
- A social media campaign for the partnership.
- Branding at the event, on the shared partner screens and signage.
- One article in the ClimbUK newsletter, written by the partner.
- A dedicated Partner Manager, backed by a support team.
- An onboarding platform that does the work for them: the tasks are listed and the templates are ready, so they answer the questions and they are set up, with priority support.
- An online portal where they manage their app profile (improved for Climb27), their tickets and attendees, and, new for Climb27, their leads.
- In the ClimbGroup Event App: their partner profile and a place in the exhibitor and partner lists, a pin on the event map, their booth staff listed so attendees can book appointments with them, appointment management, access to their leads, and engagement reporting (the attendees who engaged with them in the app, and what they did).

**Headline Partners also get:**

- More social posts, website features and newsletter features.
- Their logo on every ClimbUK social post, all paid advertising and every newsletter.
- A bigger space, and first choice of location within reason.
- The title of Headline Partner, and the standing that comes with it.

**In the ClimbGroup Event App.** The profile, lists, map pin, booth staff, appointments, leads and reporting above are included with every partnership; everything below is an option to add. Always call it the ClimbGroup Event App, never by the platform it runs on.

- **Featured listing:** a place among up to eight featured partners on the app's home page, with videos, documents and links on their listing, and chat, messages, appointments and a one-to-one video meeting room with attendees.
- **Sponsored sessions:** their name and logo on a session in the agenda, with polls and surveys, and the leads from the people who took part.
- **Page banners:** a banner, or a place in a rotating set, at the top of the home, exhibitor or partner pages, linking to their listing, an app page or their website, with a click report after the event.
- **Event ads:** ads on the home, exhibitor and session pages, targeted by attendee type, with an impressions and clicks report.
- **Video ads:** a short advert that plays while attendees wait for a session to start.
- **Home page link and video cards:** up to three links or videos in one card on the home page.
- **The app game:** a challenge themed to the partner, worth extra points, so attendees visit their stand.
- **Discussions:** a text or video discussion with their name on it, on a topic they lead.
- **Notifications:** promotional messages sent between sessions, to all attendees or a chosen group.
- **Custom page:** a page in the app built for the partner and their campaign.
- **Splash screen:** the full-screen image every attendee sees when they open the app. Exclusive to one partner, and priced to match.
- **Home page and login images:** the partner in the home page cover image, the event details header or the login screen, priced by whether one partner or several share it.
- **Lead capture:** scanning and capturing leads at their stand.

**On the session screens and the ticket.** ClimbUK runs these itself in the rooms:

- **Sponsored sessions:** the partner's logo on screen for the session.
- **Video ads:** a partner video on the room screens before or after a session.
- **Session intros and outros:** a custom intro or outro effect or video for a session, supplied by the partner.
- **The ticket:** the partner's brand on the digital ticket, the QR code every attendee shows to get in.

Describe these as options to build from, like the rest of a partnership. Never sell them as fixed Silver, Gold or Platinum bundles.

**Multi-year partners.** NatWest announced a three-year partnership at Climb26, so it is a key partner for Climb27, Climb28 and Climb29. A partner brochure can give it a section of its own as the example of a long-term partnership, using the NatWest stand photos.

## Sales brochures

- **Format:** 16:9 pages, sent as a PDF or shown on screen in a call.
- **Look:** photo-led, like the Climb26 exhibitor brochure. Big uppercase lines in orange label boxes, stands shown as renders with a round size badge, prices in a solid box, and real quotes from people who came, with their name and company.
- **Partnerships are options.** Every partnership is built around what the partner wants. Show the level (Headline Partner or Partner), the things a partner can own and the extras they can add, then invite a call. Never fixed tiers or long text pages. The only figures are channel reach and follow-through, as above.
- **Every page earns its place:** one idea per page, one photo wall or render, and a clear next step on the last page.
- **Vary the grounds.** Dark Brown on two pages at most, and never two pages in a row on the same colour.
- **At least 20 testimonials** in each document, real and attributed, and never the same quote twice in one document. Spread the voices: two quotes from the same person or organisation never sit on the same page or on pages next to each other.
- **Plenty of exhibitors.** Fill the photo walls with exhibitors and their stands, and show a real photo of each stand type.
- **A clear closing page.** The QR code sits centred in its own white card, beside the headline, the button and the contact details.
- **Reading text never sits on orange.** Pages with body copy, lists or quotes use White, Light Grey, Charcoal or Dark Brown. Keep full orange pages for the cover moment and the closing call to action, where only big white lines sit on the orange.
- **Cards stand out from their page.** On a dark page a card is one step lighter (Charcoal on Dark Brown) with a hairline; never a card the same colour as the page.
- **Cards in a row line up.** Every card in a row has a short description, the same height and its price on one shared line at the bottom.
- **Lists are read at a glance.** List text is at least 24px on a 1920px page, sized to match its arrow or icon, with clear space between the list and any quote box below it.
- **Each stand type gets its own photos.** The starter pod is shown with a plain render, not another company's custom pods (those photos suit the expo stand). Pod photos go on the pod page and nowhere else.
- **The marketplace is for makers and new businesses.** Consultants and service firms belong in the starter pods, not at a marketplace table. Social enterprises and community organisations get a marketplace option of their own.
- **The marketplace page shows the festival.** Six or seven busy festival photos (packed rooms, arrivals, networking), not more stand shots.
- **Photos match the page.** A page about the festival or the stage uses stage, awards and crowd photos, not a speaker on a stand. Never put two photos of the same stand or organisation side by side, and mix the organisations across a photo wall.
- **Name what is sold.** Partners buy workshops, roundtables, ClimbAwards and zones. ClimbUK does not sell main stage sessions, so never head these offers "stage" or "take the stage"; call them sessions and awards.
- **Keep the story in the crop.** A conversation keeps both people in frame, so never crop a two-person shot down to one side. A photo that shows branding (a zone panel, a partner sign, a screen) keeps the whole sign and its logos in frame. Give it a tile of its own shape, or pick a landscape shot, rather than zooming a tall sign into a wide tile.
- **Icon cards are centred and short.** The icon, title and text are centred, and the text is two lines at most: cut anything longer. The icon is part of the card's design, large and in Climb Orange (on a soft Orange Tint disc, or as an orange pin over the card's top edge), not a small mark in the corner.
- **Photo cards carry no icons.** A card with a photo at the top has its title and text only; icons are for cards without a photo.
- **Quotes and photos get their own pages.** Put the quotes together on one quotes page and give the photos a full page of their own, rather than splitting both across two mixed pages.
- **No empty corners.** Every page fills its frame down to the footer. When a column ends early, extend the photo wall, enlarge the photo or add another quote; never leave a blank block under a card or a quote.
- **Quotes have room.** Leave clear space above and below every quote box, so it never touches a list or a paragraph.
- **Explain each partner level.** Say what Headline Partner and Partner each give in a full sentence or two, not a name on its own.
- **Show everything a partner can own or add.** For example printed panels, logos on screens, branding a space and naming a space. Each one costs extra, and all of them are options to build from. Describe each option by what the partner gets, not only where their logo goes:
  - **The event app:** advertising in the ClimbGroup Event App, with dedicated sections and lead capture, as well as their brand.
  - **Badges and lanyards:** their logo on every attendee badge, and for a large enough partnership, lanyards made for the event with their logo.
  - **A zone:** their name and branding across one zone, end to end (the Climb26 University Zone with Barclays is the example).
  - **The VIP space:** the VIP space and the VIP badges together (the Climb26 JCT600 Porsche VIP Suite is the example).
  - **The kiosks:** the kiosk podiums are exclusive to one partner. Logos on the kiosk screens are sold separately, to as many partners as want them.
  - Show each option with a real Climb26 photo of it, never an older edition.
- **The closing page is three clear blocks:** the headline in square label boxes, the round button under it, and a card below holding the contact lines (centred, the web address written in full: www.climb-uk.com/partnerships), beside the QR card. One logo only: Climb27 (or its tagline lockup), never side by side with ClimbUK. Keep it uncluttered.
